DUBAI DESERT SAFARI & CAMEL EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of golden sand dunes, breathtaking sunsets and unforgettable adventures? Join us for an exciting day in the Dubai Desert, combining thrilling activities with traditional Emirati culture.


Stop 1: Desert Safari Adventure

  • Experience an exhilarating 4x4 dune-bashing ride across the golden desert.
  • Try sandboarding down Dubai's famous sand dunes.
  • Admire the stunning desert landscapes and endless views.
  • Capture incredible photographs at scenic desert viewpoints.

Stop 2: Camel Ride Experience
  • Enjoy a traditional camel ride through the Arabian Desert.
  • Learn about the importance of camels in Emirati culture and history.
  • Watch a breathtaking desert sunset over the dunes.
  • Relax at a traditional desert camp and experience local hospitality.

Price = €109 pp

UAE THRILLS & LUXURY ADVENTURE

Dreaming of adrenaline-filled experiences, luxury attractions and unforgettable views? Join us for an action-packed 5-day UAE adventure, featuring world-famous theme parks, luxury experiences and breathtaking aerial views of Dubai.


Day 1: Ferrari World Abu Dhabi

  • Experience thrilling rides, including Formula Rossa, one of the world's fastest roller coasters.
  • Explore Ferrari-themed attractions and interactive racing experiences.

Day 2: Atlantis Aquaventure Waterpark
  • Enjoy record-breaking water slides and exciting attractions.
  • Relax on beautiful beaches and swimming areas.

Day 3: Dubai Yacht Cruise
  • Cruise through Dubai Marina aboard a luxury yacht.
  • Admire stunning views of Palm Jumeirah and Dubai's skyline.

Day 4: Skydive Dubai
  • Experience a tandem skydive over the iconic Palm Jumeirah.
  • Capture unforgettable aerial views and photos of Dubai.

Day 5: Desert Safari Adventure
  • Enjoy an exciting 4x4 dune-bashing experience through the desert.
  • Take a traditional camel ride and watch the sunset over the dunnes.

Price = €899 pp
