DESTINATIONS

Four continents, eight countries. Pick a place and see what it offers.

Watch and listen

Europe

Spain

Capital: Madrid | Currency: Euro | Language: Spanish | Best time: April to October

Barcelona (Sagrada Família)

A lively coastal city famous for Gaudi’s buildings, sandy beaches and tapas bars.

Granada (the Alhambra)

A hilltop city in southern Spain, home to the Alhambra palace and its beautiful old streets.

France

Capital: Paris | Currency: Euro | Language: French | Best time: April to October

Paris (Eiffel Tower)

The capital of France, known for the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre museum and its cafes.

Nice, French Riviera

A sunny city on the French Riviera with pebble beaches, a seaside walk and a colourful old town.

Asia

Japan

Capital: Tokyo | Currency: Yen | Language: Japanese | Best time: March to May

Tokyo ( Senso-ji)

A huge, busy city where bright neon streets sit next to calm temples and gardens.

Kyoto (Fushimi Inari)

Japan’s old capital, full of temples, shrines and traditional streets.

United Arab Emirates

Capital: Abu Dhabi | Currency: Dirham | Language: Arabic | Best time: November to March

Dubai (Burj Khalifa)

A modern city with record-breaking skyscrapers like the Burj Khalifa, huge malls and desert trips.

Abu Dhabi (Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque)

The capital of the UAE, home to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and its white marble domes.

Africa

Egypt

Capital: Cairo | Currency: Egyptian pound | Language: Arabic | Best time: October to April

Giza (the Pyramids)

A big, busy city beside the Great Pyramids and the Sphinx, which are thousands of years old.

Luxor (Valley of the Kings, temples)

Often called the world’s greatest open-air museum, with giant temples and the Valley of the Kings.

South Africa

Capital: Pretoria | Currency: Rand | Language: English and 11 others | Best time: September to November

Cape Town (Table Mountain)

A coastal city under Table Mountain, with beaches, vineyards and great views.

Kruger National Park (safari)

One of Africa’s best safari parks, where you can see lions, elephants and rhinos.

South America

Peru

Capital: Lima | Currency: Sol | Language: Spanish | Best time: May to September

Machu Picchu

The old Inca capital and the ancient mountain city nearby, one of the most famous sights in the world.

Lima (food, coastline)

Peru’s capital on the Pacific coast, well known for its food and ocean views.

Brazil

Capital: Brasilia | Currency: Real | Language: Portuguese | Best time: May to October

Rio de Janeiro

A beach city famous for Christ the Redeemer, Copacabana beach and its carnival.

Iguazu Falls

A huge group of waterfalls surrounded by rainforest on the border with Argentina.
