MACHU PICCHU TREK & CUSCO EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of breathtaking mountain landscapes, ancient Incan history and unforgettable adventures? Join us for a 6-day Peruvian getaway, featuring a 4-day Machu Picchu trek and 2 days exploring the historic city of Cusco, the former capital of the Inca Empire.


Day 1: Cusco Discovery

  • Explore Cusco's historic centre and beautiful colonial architecture.
  • Visit the bustling Plaza de Armas and local artisan markets.
  • Learn about the city's rich Incan and Spanish heritage.
  • Enjoy traditional Peruvian cuisine and cultural experiences.

Day 2: Cusco & Sacred Valley
  • Visit the stunning Sacred Valley and admire breathtaking scenery.
  • Explore ancient Incan ruins and traditional Andean villages.
  • Discover the history and traditions of the Inca civilisation.
  • Capture incredible photos of the surrounding mountains.

Day 3: Machu Picchu Trek Begins
  • Start your trek through spectacular Andean landscapes.
  • Pass ancient archaeological sites along the route.
  • Camp overnight surrounded by breathtaking scenery.

Day 4: Trek Through the Andes
  • Hike through cloud forests and mountain passes.
  • Experience some of the most scenic sections of the trail.
  • Learn about local wildlife and ecosystems.

Day 5: Final Trek to Machu Picchu
  • Continue along historic Incan pathways.
  • Reach the iconic Machu Picchu archaeological site.
  • Celebrate completing the legendary trek.

Day 6: Explore Machu Picchu
  • Enjoy a guided tour of the Lost City of the Incas.
  • Learn about the fascinating history of this UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Capture unforgettable photographs of the ancient ruins.
  • Return with memories of a once-in-a-lifetime adventure.

Price = €999 pp

HUACACHINA DESERT & BALLESTAS ISLANDS ADVENTURE

Dreaming of incredible wildlife, thrilling adventures and breathtaking scenery? Join us for an unforgettable day exploring Peru's famous Ballestas Islands and the spectacular desert oasis of Huacachina.


Stop 1: Ballestas Islands

  • Take a boat tour around the stunning Ballestas Islands.
  • Spot sea lions, penguins and a variety of marine birds.
  • Admire dramatic coastal cliffs and rock formations.
  • Learn about Peru's unique coastal wildlife.

Stop 2: Huacachina Oasis
  • Visit the breathtaking desert oasis surrounded by towering sand dunes.
  • Experience an exhilarating dune buggy ride across the desert.
  • Try sandboarding on some of South America's largest dunes.
  • Watch a spectacular sunset over the golden desert landscape.

Price = €139 pp
