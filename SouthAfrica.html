SOUTH AFRICA SAFARI EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of incredible wildlife, breathtaking landscapes and unforgettable adventures? Join us for a 2-day South African Safari Experience, featuring exciting game drives and an overnight stay overlooking a spectacular watering hole where animals gather throughout the day and night.


Day 1: Safari Adventure

  • Enjoy a guided game drive in search of lions, elephants, rhinos and other amazing wildlife.
  • Learn about South Africa's diverse ecosystems from experienced safari guides.
  • Relax at your luxury lodge surrounded by stunning African scenery.
  • Watch animals gather at the watering hole as the sun sets and the stars appear.

Day 2: Sunrise Safari
  • Experience an early morning game drive when wildlife is most active.
  • Spot a variety of animals as they begin their day in the African bush.
  • Capture incredible photographs of the breathtaking landscapes and wildlife.
  • Enjoy breakfast overlooking the watering hole before departing.

Price = €399

CAPE TOWN SURF & CITY EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of golden beaches, breathtaking scenery and exciting outdoor adventures? Join us for an unforgettable day exploring Cape Town, one of South Africa's most beautiful cities, with the opportunity to learn how to surf on its stunning coastline.


Stop 1: Cape Town City Highlights

  • Explore the vibrant city centre and discover Cape Town's rich culture and history.
  • Take in spectacular views of the iconic Table Mountain.
  • Visit the lively Victoria & Alfred Waterfront.
  • Enjoy free time to capture photos and explore local shops.

Stop 2: Learn to Surf
  • Take part in a beginner-friendly surf lesson with professional instructors.
  • Learn essential surfing techniques and water safety skills.
  • Put your new skills into practice on Cape Town's beautiful beaches.
  • Enjoy the thrill of catching your first wave.

Stop 3: Coastal Scenic Drive
  • Travel along Cape Town's breathtaking coastline.
  • Admire stunning ocean views and dramatic cliffs.
  • Stop at scenic viewpoints for unforgettable photographs.
  • Relax and enjoy the beauty of South Africa's coast.

Price = €109 pp
