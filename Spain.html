SIERRA NEVADA SKI TRIP

Ready for an unforgettable winter adventure? Join us for a thrilling 3-day getaway to Sierra Nevada, Spain, home to some of Europe's highest ski slopes and stunning mountain scenery.


What to expect:

Day 1:
  • Arrive in Sierra Nevada and check into your accommodation.
  • Collect ski equipment and lift passes.
  • Enjoy free time exploring the resort village.

Day 2:
  • Spend a full day skiing or snowboarding on Spain's famous slopes.
  • Suitable runs available for beginners, intermediates, and advanced skiers.
  • Enjoy lunch at a mountain restaurant with panoramic views.
  • Experience the lively après-ski atmosphere in the evening.

Day 3:
  • Enjoy a final morning on the slopes.
  • Capture unforgettable photos of the breathtaking scenery.
  • Relax before departure and journey home.

CAMINO DE SANTIAGO 5-DAY ADVENTURE

Dreaming of breathtaking scenery, historic towns and an unforgettable walking journey? Join us for a 5-day experience along the famous Camino de Santiago, one of Europe's most iconic pilgrimage routes, filled with culture, history and stunning landscapes.


Day 1: Arrival in Sarria

  • Explore the historic town of Sarria and its charming streets.
  • Collect your pilgrim passport and prepare for the Camino journey.

Day 2: Walk to Portomarín
  • Hike through beautiful Galician countryside and woodland trails.
  • Visit the picturesque town of Portomarín and its historic church.

Day 3: Walk to Palas de Rei
  • Enjoy peaceful rural landscapes and traditional villages.
  • Experience authentic Galician culture along the route.

Day 4: Walk to Arzúa
  • Continue through rolling green hills and scenic pathways.
  • Sample local cuisine and relax in the welcoming pilgrim atmosphere.

Day 5: Santiago de Compostela
  • Arrive at the magnificent Santiago de Compostela Cathedral.
  • Receive your Camino certificate and celebrate your achievement.

Price = €499pp
