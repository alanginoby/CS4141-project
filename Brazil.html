RIO FAVELA & CITY EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of vibrant culture, breathtaking views and authentic local experiences? Join us for an unforgettable day exploring the heart of Rio de Janeiro, including a guided visit to one of the city's famous favelas and some of Rio's most iconic sights.


Stop 1: Favela Community Tour

  • Take a guided walking tour through a Rio favela and learn about its history and culture.
  • Meet local residents and discover community-led projects and businesses.
  • Learn about daily life and the challenges and achievements of favela communities.
  • Enjoy incredible views of Rio from unique hillside viewpoints.

Stop 2: Christ the Redeemer
  • Visit the world-famous Christ the Redeemer statue.
  • Take in panoramic views of Rio de Janeiro and Guanabara Bay.
  • Learn about one of Brazil's most iconic landmarks.
  • Capture unforgettable photos from the summit.

Stop 3: Copacabana Beach
  • Relax on Rio's famous Copacabana Beach.
  • Enjoy the lively atmosphere and stunning coastline.
  • Explore local cafés, shops and beachside markets.
  • Take in the beauty of Rio's golden sands before departure.

      BRAZIL ADVENTURE EXPERIENCE

      Dreaming of tropical beaches, thrilling adventures and unforgettable wildlife encounters? Join us for an action-packed 3-day Brazilian adventure, combining natural wonders, outdoor activities and breathtaking scenery.


      Day 1: Iguazu Falls Adventure

      • Visit the stunning Iguazu Falls, one of the largest waterfall systems in the world.
      • Walk scenic trails and viewing platforms overlooking the falls.
      • Spot local wildlife including toucans and coatis.
      • Capture incredible photographs of the cascading waterfalls.

      Day 2: Surf & Coastal Adventure
      • Learn to surf with a professional instructor on one of Rio's beautiful beaches.
      • Practice your skills in the warm waters of the Atlantic Ocean.
      • Enjoy a scenic coastal tour along Rio's stunning shoreline.
      • Explore local cafés and beachside markets.

      Day 3: Tijuca Rainforest Adventure
      • Hike through the world's largest urban rainforest, Tijuca National Park.
      • Discover waterfalls, wildlife and breathtaking viewpoints.
      • Learn about Brazil's unique plants and ecosystems.
      • Capture spectacular photos overlooking Rio and the coastline.
