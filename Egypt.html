PYRAMIDS & HOT AIR BALLOON EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of ancient wonders, breathtaking desert landscapes and unforgettable views? Join us for an incredible day exploring Egypt's famous pyramids, combined with a spectacular hot air balloon ride for a truly once-in-a-lifetime experience.


Stop 1: The Great Pyramids of Giza

  • Visit the world-famous Pyramids of Giza, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
  • Learn about the fascinating history of the ancient Egyptian pharaohs.
  • Admire the incredible architecture and engineering of the pyramids.
  • Capture unforgettable photographs of these iconic landmarks.

Stop 2: The Great Sphinx
  • Discover the mysterious Great Sphinx of Giza.
  • Learn about the legends and history surrounding this ancient monument.
  • Explore the surrounding archaeological site.
  • Enjoy free time to take photos and soak in the atmosphere.

Stop 3: Hot Air Balloon Experience
  • Take to the skies on a breathtaking hot air balloon ride.
  • Enjoy panoramic views of Egypt's stunning landscapes from above.
  • Watch the sunrise illuminate the desert and historic sites below.
    • Create unforgettable memories with one of Egypt's most spectacular experiences.

EGYPT ADVENTURE & SCUBA DIVING EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of crystal-clear waters, ancient history and unforgettable adventures? Join us for an exciting 3-day Egyptian getaway, combining world-famous landmarks, incredible marine life and unique cultural experiences.


Day 1: Cairo & Egyptian History

  • Visit the world-famous Egyptian Museum and discover ancient treasures.
  • Learn about Egypt's fascinating history and legendary pharaohs.
  • Explore the vibrant streets and markets of Cairo.
  • Capture unforgettable photos of the city's iconic landmarks.

Day 2: Red Sea Scuba Diving
  • Enjoy a guided scuba diving experience in the crystal-clear Red Sea.
  • Discover colourful coral reefs and a variety of exotic marine life.
  • Receive instruction from experienced diving professionals.
  • Relax on beautiful sandy beaches between diving sessions.

Day 3: Desert & Coastal Adventure
  • Experience a guided desert safari through Egypt's stunning landscapes.
  • Visit traditional local communities and learn about their culture.
  • Enjoy breathtaking views of the desert and coastline.
  • Celebrate your final day with a relaxing seaside dinner.

Price = €299 pp
