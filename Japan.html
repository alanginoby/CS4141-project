TOKYO CITY & FOOD EXPERIENCE

Dreaming of vibrant city life, incredible cuisine and unforgettable cultural experiences? Join us for an exciting day exploring the best of Tokyo, combining iconic landmarks with Japan's world-famous food scene.


Stop 1: Tokyo City Highlights

  • Experience the energy of the famous Shibuya Crossing, one of the busiest intersections in the world.
  • Visit the vibrant districts of Harajuku and Shinjuku.
  • Take in breathtaking views from the Tokyo Skytree.
  • Explore Tokyo's unique blend of modern innovation and traditional culture.

Stop 2: Tokyo Food Experience
  • Visit traditional food markets and discover authentic Japanese cuisine.
  • Learn how to make sushi with guidance from local experts.
  • Sample local favourites including sushi, ramen and mochi.
  • Experience the flavours that have made Japan one of the world's top culinary destinations.

Price = €119 pp

JAPAN CULTURE & ISLAND ADVENTURE

Dreaming of ancient temples, breathtaking scenery and unforgettable cultural experiences? Join us for an exciting 3-day Japanese adventure, combining historic cities, beautiful islands and some of Japan's most famous landmarks.


Day 1: Kyoto & Nara Experience

  • Visit Kyoto's stunning temples and traditional streets.
  • Walk through the famous Arashiyama Bamboo Forest.
  • Meet the friendly deer roaming freely in Nara Park.
  • Discover the rich history and culture of ancient Japan.

Day 2: Hiroshima Discovery
  • Visit the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park and Museum.
  • Learn about Japan's modern history and resilience.
  • Explore the vibrant city centre and local markets.
  • Enjoy authentic Japanese cuisine and cultural attractions.

Day 3: Miyajima Island Adventure
  • Take a ferry to the beautiful island of Miyajima.
  • See the world-famous floating torii gate of Itsukushima Shrine.
  • Hike scenic trails with stunning views of the Seto Inland Sea.
  • Spot the island's famous free-roaming deer and explore traditional streets.

Price = €449 pp
